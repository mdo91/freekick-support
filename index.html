How to play

Start a game and take ten free kicks from ten positions. Begin your swipe inside the ring around the ball. Swipe upward to set power: a longer stroke makes a stronger shot. Angle the stroke to aim, and bend the swipe to curve the ball. Release to shoot, or return to the starting point before releasing to cancel.

Goals earn 3–10 points based on distance, angle, and the wall. Complete a round to save your points, goals, and best streak. Choose one of four balls from the start screen.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game need internet access?

No. Gameplay and all game assets work offline. Support, privacy, and asset-credit links open external websites when you choose to visit them.

Where is my progress saved?

Your selected ball and most recent completed game are stored on your device. There is no FreeKick account or online leaderboard. Deleting the app removes its local data; system backups may retain copies according to your Apple settings.

Why is there no sound?

FreeKick respects silent mode. Check the silent switch or Action button setting and your media volume. Resume the game after an audio interruption or headphone disconnection.

How do I change the language?

FreeKick follows your preferred supported iOS language. You can also select an app language in iOS Settings when multiple preferred languages are configured. Supported languages: English, Japanese, Korean, Turkish, Russian, German, Ukrainian, Portuguese (Brazil), and Spanish.

How do I pause or return home?

Tap Pause to stop the action and Resume to continue. Home ends the current round and returns to the start screen. Your last completed game and ball selection remain saved.
