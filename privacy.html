Privacy policy for FreeKick: Training Club

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Support

Who operates FreeKick

FreeKick: Training Club is an offline football game developed by Mahmut Aoata. This policy describes version 1.0 of the iPhone and iPad app.

Data collection and tracking

The FreeKick app does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer or third parties. It has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking, online leaderboard, or in-app purchases. Gameplay does not require a network connection.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo library.

Data stored on your device

FreeKick stores your selected ball and the results of your most recent completed game, including shot outcomes and awarded points, in the app's local preferences. Goals and the longest streak are derived from those saved results. These values are used only to restore your selection and show your last game.

The developer cannot access this saved game data. Deleting the app removes its local data. Device backups and restores are managed by Apple and your device settings; a backup may retain or restore app data.

External links and this website

The app includes optional links to support, this privacy policy, and third-party asset credits and licenses. These links open websites outside the game. Those sites may process information under their own privacy policies.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, such as IP addresses, to provide and secure its services. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This website adds no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Support requests

If you choose to open a support request on GitHub, the account information and content you post are processed by GitHub and visible to the developer and the public. Share only what is needed to describe the issue. Requests can be managed through GitHub's available editing and deletion controls.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from players, including children. Support requests and external websites are separate services subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact Mahmut Aoata through the FreeKick support repository. Do not post sensitive information in a public issue.
